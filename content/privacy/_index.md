## Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information GeoSim3D collects through geosim3d.com and
demo.geosim3d.com, and how we use it.

### Website and demo visits

We use Google Analytics to count visits and to see how the site and live demo are used,
for example which pages are viewed and whether the demo loads. This relies on cookies and
records general information such as your browser, device type, and approximate location.
It does not tell us who you are. We also use Google Ads measurement to see whether our ads
lead to contact requests.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics
with Google's [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Contact form

When you send us a message, we receive what you enter in the form, such as your name,
email address, company, and message. The form is delivered to us by Web3Forms. We use this
information only to reply to you and to discuss your project.

### Project data

Drawings, layouts, track plans, and other files you share with us for a project are used
only for that project. We don't show them publicly or in our own demos without your
permission, and we're happy to sign an NDA.

### Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only with the services
named above that help run this site, or if the law requires it.

### Your choices

To ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email
[info@geosim3d.com](mailto:info@geosim3d.com).

### Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
