## Our Story

GeoSim3D grew out of vehicle dynamics research at Virginia Tech's Advanced Vehicle Dynamics Laboratory, and a fascination with vehicles, trains in particular. That grew into more than 15 years of modeling and simulation work on defense, aerospace, and commercial projects, including software shipped on the App Store and Google Play.

We bring that experience to transit: digital twins that make rail and transit operations safer and more efficient, for operators and riders alike.

## How We Work

We start from the data you already have (track plans, CAD drawings, survey and lidar data, aerial imagery) and turn it into a working tool: a rail yard you can switch trains in, a corridor you can ride, an airfield you can plan around. Each one is built for training, planning, and day-to-day operations, and built with real care for how it looks.
